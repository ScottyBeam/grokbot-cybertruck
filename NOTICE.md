## No vehicle connection

Nothing here connects to a vehicle. There is no app, no API client, no authentication,
no token handling, and no code of any kind.

What this repository contains is nine prompts, a rulebook, and templates for the
numbers you type in yourself. Your vehicle is not involved, and no credentials are ever
requested or stored.

## Not a driving aid or safety system

Every number this setup produces is an estimate, generated by a language model from
figures a person typed in by hand.

It is **not** a navigation system, not a range guarantee, not a maintenance authority,
and not a safety device.

Do not use it to decide whether a vehicle is safe to drive, whether a route is
passable, whether you will reach a destination, or whether a component needs service.
Use your vehicle's own instruments, the manufacturer's guidance, and your own
judgement for all of that.

The SERVICE agent's veto is a prompt being cautious, not an inspection. A veto is a
reason to go and look at the thing it named. The **absence** of a veto means nothing at
all about your vehicle's condition.

Language models produce confident wrong answers. This setup is designed to make its
reasoning visible so you can catch that — not to be trusted without checking.

## Not validated

No prediction in this repository has been checked against a real outcome over a
meaningful number of trips. "Honest figure" here means *honestly derived from the
inputs given*, not *verified accurate*.

[SETUP.md](SETUP.md) asks you to run this alongside your own instruments for a week
before relying on it. That instruction is there for a reason.

## No performance claims

This project makes no claim to improve your range, save you money, extend the life of
any component, or make any drive safer. It is a way of organising a decision, published
